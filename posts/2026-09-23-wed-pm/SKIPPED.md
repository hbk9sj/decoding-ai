# 2026-09-23 wed-pm — skipped on purpose

The two-a-day schedule started on 23 Sep 2026. That day's two posts (the tue and wed
folders) had already gone out at 09:15 IST, under the old one-a-day slots. A 20:00 IST post
would sit under 12 h after them, so the spacing rule would push it to Thursday 08:00, push
Thursday's morning post to the evening, and so on down the week.

This folder exists so the pm routine sees the slot as done and stops. It has no copy.json,
so the worker and verify.sh pass over it.
